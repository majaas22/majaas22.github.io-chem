Overview of the Model

The python simulation models how concentrations of chemical species change over time in a closed reaction system. Three simulations were developed: the first models a simple one step reversible reaction A ⇌ B, the second introduces anirreversible step B → C to create a multistep reaction mechanism and the third further extends the model by taking into consideration the effect of temperature using the Arrhenius equation.
