Model and Method

Overview of the Model

The python simulation models how concentrations of chemical species change over time in a closed reaction system. Three simulations were developed: the first models a simple one step reversible reaction A ⇌ B, the second introduces an irreversible step B → C to create a multistep reaction mechanism and the third further extends the model by taking into consideration the effect of temperature using the Arrhenius equation.

In these simulations, time is treated as the independent variable while the concentrations of each chemical species are the dependent variables. The control variables consist of the rate constants, time step size (Δt), initial concentrations of the chemical species and temperature (in simulations one and two). As a result a set of values, showing how the concentration values change over time, is obtained. This is the output of the simulation and the values are then used to generate concentration-time graphs for each of the reactions.
