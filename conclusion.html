Conclusion

Overall Findings

All three simulations successfully showed how different factors can influence the behaviour of a reaction system. Simulation 1 demonstrated that a reversible reaction can reach dynamic equilibrium with the concentrations of the chemical species becoming constant towards the end of the reaction. Simulation 2 introduced an irreversible step which continuously removed the intermediate to produce the final product. Simulation 3 investigated how a change in temperature affects the chemical system and concluded that increasing temperature increases the rate of reaction allowing the reaction to reach its final state faster. Overall, all three simulations were consistent with the expected principles of chemical kinetics and supported the general trends that were predicted by the hypotheses.
